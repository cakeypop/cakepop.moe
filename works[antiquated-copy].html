hardware

Hydrophone Preamp PCB

Designed a 4-channel JFET hydrophone preamplifier and ADC board from scratch, adapting a reference topology (Alex Rice / Zach Poff) into a full production design in KiCad. Takes signal from four Aquarian AS-1 hydrophones, preamplifies through discrete JFET stages, and digitizes all four channels synchronously through a PCM1865 ADC over I²S to the Jetson.

Went through several major iterations over months: hand sketch → breadboard prototype validating the analog stage → recurring design reviews with Dr. Furth (former EE professor) to pressure-test the circuit → final board, split into mother and daughter boards to isolate analog/digital signal domains and keep the noise floor clean.

  • Reverse-polarity protection, power/COMM status LEDs, resettable fuse, 0Ω test points for troubleshooting
  • Fabricated through JLCPCB (5-board min. run), including their DFM verification process
  • Hand-soldered all non-SMD components, bring-up tested before competition
  • I²S data path validated end-to-end using a UMC404HD as a Jetson test-bench substitute
Board photos incoming physical board + KiCad renders to be added. Full TDOA validation planned once the sub is back in the lab.

Competition Execution

Working autonomy wasn't ready at competition. Rather than scratch the run, wrote a script to hard-code a command sequence that let the vehicle execute a qualifying run without full autonomy — on an average of two hours of sleep a night. In the days before travel, pulled in a friend (Wen, outside the team) to build and test computer-vision scripts at the NMSU pool. Autonomy didn't make the deadline; the qualifying run held.

Maintained detailed project logs since September 2025 and organized the team's datasheet archive; currently building a continuity log so next year's capstone team can pick up the project without starting from zero.
