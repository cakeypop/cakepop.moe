Rubik's Cube Simulation Project

The above video is a demo showcasing a project that simulates a Rubik's Cube using an Arduino Uno with an LCD shield attached.
In the video, I send a series of inputs(turns) to the program, that manipulates the cube from a solved state to the pattern matching the physical cube on the webcam.
The pattern gives the illusion of a cube inside of a cube inside of a cube, and was used just for fun.
As the inputs are sent to the Arduino Uno, the LCD screen updates both the 2D and 3D map of the virtual cube.
