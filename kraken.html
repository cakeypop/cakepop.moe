01 About the Team

A first-year team competing
in the RoboSub 2026 Competition

Kraken Submersibles is New Mexico State University's inaugural entry into the RoboSub competition — an international challenge where teams design fully autonomous underwater vehicles to navigate gates, identify markers, and engage targets without any surface communication.

Founded through the NMSU College of Engineering Capstone program, our team brings together students across electrical, mechanical, and computer engineering disciplines. We work out of dedicated lab space in Goddard Hall and collaborate with established programs at ASU and SDSU as we build the foundation for years of NMSU competition entries to come.

03 Subsystems

Engineered, end-to-end.

Every layer of the Nautilus, including machined aluminum, computer vision inference, and a custom designed circuit, are all designed and integrated by our team.

MECHANICAL

Frame & Hull

An open aluminum-extrusion chassis houses the pressure vessel, electronics bays, thrusters, and payload mounts. Designed for neutral buoyancy and serviceability between competition runs.

  • Custom aluminum frame
  • Acrylic / aluminum pressure vessel
  • Modular electronics enclosures
  • Integrated grasper & torpedo launchers
ELECTRICAL

Power & Signal

Dual 4S LiPo batteries feed a multi-rail bus distributed through a Holybro PM07. Custom PCBs handle hydrophone amplification, ADC, and sensor integration.

  • Dual 4S LiPo · 236 Wh usable
  • Nautilus PCB: 4-ch hydrophone amp + PCM1865 ADC
  • Blue Sea ML-RBS main relay + fuse protection
  • Phantom-powered AS-1 hydrophone array
SOFTWARE & AUTONOMY

Perception & Control

YOLOv9 vision on a CUDA-accelerated Jetson Orin Nano runs alongside ArduSub flight control, fused with DVL navigation and acoustic localization via TDOA on the hydrophone array.

  • Jetson Orin Nano · OpenCV w/ CUDA
  • YOLOv9 underwater object detection
  • MAVLink ↔ ArduSub over USB-UART
  • TDOA acoustic localization (44.1 kHz, 4 ch)
ACOUSTICS

Hydrophone Array

A four-element AS-1 hydrophone array feeds discrete JFET preamps based on the Alex Rice/Zach Poff topology into our in-house Nautilus PCB for synchronous 4-channel digitization.

  • 4× Aquarian AS-1 hydrophones
  • JFET preamplifier (2SK209)
  • PCM1865 quad-channel ADC over I²S
  • 24 V phantom power rail
